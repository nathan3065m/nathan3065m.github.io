What Mozhi does

Everything the front desk would do on the phone

  • Answers missed calls, 24/7

    When the desk is busy or closed, Mozhi picks up, day and night, every day of the week.

  • Answers questions

    Opening hours, prices, services and directions, from the details the business gives Mozhi.

  • Books into the calendar

    Appointments, trial sessions, classes and table reservations go straight into the business's calendar, by staff member and service.

  • WhatsApp follow-up

    Booking confirmations, reminders and two-way chat on the app customers already use.

  • Customer history

    A simple CRM that keeps every caller, with each call's transcript and a short summary.

  • Recalls and renewals

    Reminders that bring customers back: patient recalls, membership renewals and repeat visits.

  • Follow-ups and reviews

    Follow-up messages after a visit, and review requests (kept off for clinics, as medical rules require).

  • Excel import and export

    Bring existing customer lists in from Excel, and take them out again at any time.

  • Built for India's rules

    Every call says up front that it is an AI. It never gives medical advice. Consent is recorded, and a customer's data is deleted when they ask.
